OPINION → EMOTION → REASON → EXAMPLE → IDIOM
BướcVí dụ mẫu (phe SAVE)
OPINIONTo be honest, I think young people should save more.
EMOTIONHaving savings gives me peace of mind.
REASONBecause you never know when an emergency will happen.
EXAMPLELast year my laptop died and I had money ready.
IDIOMAt the end of the day, you have to save for a rainy day.
